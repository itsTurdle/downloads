what am I looking at?

EUR/USD — euros priced in dollars. 1.1000 → 1.1050 is +50 pips.

SMA 20 / 100 — the average close of the last 20 / 100 sessions. Price above a rising average = uptrend; price crossing an average = momentum changing.

Dollar mirror — the US dollar index fund (UUP) flipped upside-down. When it moves with the price line, the dollar side is driving EUR/USD. When they split, something euro-side is happening.

5d / 20d move — net pips over the last 5 / 20 sessions. Recent momentum, short and medium.

vol 20d — how violently it's been moving lately (annualized). High vol = bigger swings both ways, wilder rides.

corr vs USD↓ 60d — how tightly the last 60 sessions tracked the inverted dollar. 1.00 = lockstep with the dollar, near 0 = something else is in charge.

US 10Y Δ20d — the change in the US 10-year yield over 20 sessions. Rising US yields tend to pull money into dollars (EUR/USD down); falling, the reverse.

house rules & data sources

Each round drops you at a hidden date between Feb 2007 and Aug 2026. WAIT walks the tape one session while you stay flat — wait as long as you like. LONG / SHORT enters at the current session; ride with NEXT SESSION and book with EXIT whenever you choose (a round caps at 120 sessions and auto-books). Pass ends the round without a trade. Dates unseal only when the round ends.

The coin-flip zone is the 95% range a pure guesser's hit rate lands in after the same number of trades. Every round logs its date, your action, sessions waited and held, the result in pips, and the context you saw — export the CSV and study your own tape.

Prices: ECB daily EUR/USD fixes. Dollar mirror: UUP. Yields: US 10-year constant maturity.
